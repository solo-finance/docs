An **entity** is the subject of a verification: the person or organization you
are furnishing data about or querying data for. The SOLO Network recognizes two
kinds of entities.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Consumers" icon="user">
    Individual people, identified by personal details such as name, date of birth, email, phone, and SSN.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Businesses" icon="building">
    Legal entities, identified by details such as legal name, jurisdiction of formation, registration number, and tax identifier.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every query and furnish in the network is about one entity. You never operate on
data in the abstract — you always work in the context of a specific consumer or
business.

## Consumers

A consumer represents a single individual. Consumers are matched on the
identifying details you provide:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `first_name`, `last_name` | Legal name |
| `date_of_birth` | ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| `personal_email` | |
| `phone_number` | |
| `social_security_number` | Used for high-confidence matching |

The same person is a **single profile** in the network. When you query a
consumer, the network resolves the identity you supply to that profile and
returns the data you are entitled to see for it.

<Tip>
  You don't need every field to identify a consumer, but more identifiers produce
  higher-confidence matches. SSN and date of birth are the strongest signals.
</Tip>

## Businesses

A business represents a legal entity. Businesses are identified by:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `business_legal_name` | Registered legal name |
| `business_jurisdiction_of_formation` | e.g. a US state |
| `business_registration_identifier_from_jurisdiction_of_formation` | Registration number issued at formation |
| `business_tax_identifier_type` / `business_tax_identifier_value` | e.g. `EIN` |
| `business_dba_name` | Optional "doing business as" name |
| `business_website_url` | Optional |

Like consumers, a business resolves to a single profile across the network.

## Finding an existing entity

Before furnishing or querying, you can look up entities that already exist in a
network using the search endpoints:

- `GET /entities/consumer/search` — by name, email, SSN, or date of birth
- `GET /entities/business/search` — by legal name, EIN, or email

Search is scoped to a network and returns core identity matches so you can
confirm you're acting on the right subject. See the
[API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## What you can read about an entity

Identifying an entity is not the same as being able to read its data. Two
additional rules apply:

1. **Consent** — to *query* a consumer or business, you must first record
   [consent](/concepts/consent) and obtain a `consent_id`.
2. **Entitlement** — even with consent, you only see the fields you are
   [entitled](/concepts/entitlement) to, earned by previously furnishing or
   querying that entity, and shaped by the network's
   [policies](/concepts/policies).

<Note>
  Identifying an entity tells the network *who* you mean. Consent and entitlement
  determine *what* you may read about them.
</Note>
